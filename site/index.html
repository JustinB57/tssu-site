Who We Are

We are the support staff of Penn State University — the people who keep classrooms, labs, offices, and campuses running across the Commonwealth. We're coming together with the Teamsters, one of the strongest unions in the country, to build the power that comes from standing together.

Teamsters already represent thousands of technical service workers at Penn State across more than 20 commonwealth campuses. Last year, Penn State Teamsters won their strongest contract yet. Now we want to extend those same protections to support staff.
