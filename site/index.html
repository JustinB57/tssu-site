Who We Are

We are the support staff of Penn State University — the people who keep classrooms, labs, offices, and campuses running across the Commonwealth. We're coming together with the Teamsters, one of the strongest unions in the country, to build the power that comes from standing together.

Teamsters already represent thousands of technical service workers at Penn State across more than 20 commonwealth campuses. Last year, Penn State Teamsters won their strongest contract yet. Now we want to extend those same protections to support staff.

The Union Difference

A union means we negotiate as equals — together, not alone. Here's what organizing can win for support staff:

Fair Pay

Negotiate real raises and transparent pay scales instead of leaving compensation to the discretion of management.

Job Security

A legally binding contract with protections against unfair discipline and arbitrary termination.

A Real Voice

A seat at the table on the decisions that affect your work, your schedule, and your future.

Strength in Numbers

The backing of the Teamsters and thousands of fellow Penn State workers who have done this before.

"Joining the Teamsters would benefit staff in many ways. As the prices of everything increase exponentially more than our annual salary increases, we are effectively taking pay cuts. Salary increases that even attempt to keep up with inflation can only be achieved through collective bargaining, which the Teamsters demonstrated with their most recent contract, providing all union workers with a 20 percent wage increase over four years. Additionally, the Teamsters provide job security — something we currently have none of — and advancement opportunities through bidding. I can’t wait for all of us to join the Teamsters and collectively bargain for what we deserve."

— Jessie JenkinsLibrary Services Associate at
University Park Campus
