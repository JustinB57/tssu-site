State College Pride 2026

June 13, 2026

Teamsters Support Staff Union members marched in State College Pride 2026, carrying our banner through downtown to show that support staff stand with our LGBTQ+ coworkers and community. It was a colorful, energetic day of solidarity — and a great chance to meet neighbors and spread the word about our organizing effort.
